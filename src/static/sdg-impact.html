About this prototype and how to read it

Explore how a role’s skill requirements and learning pathway may relate to selected UN targets. Each reviewed mapping includes its reasoning, supporting evidence, suggested action and limits.

Prototype mappings currently connect selected learning pathways to SDG 4.4 and SDG 8.5. Skill requirements are illustrative; other roles are not assigned targets.

Evidence availability varies by role. Employment outcomes, equitable access and broader SDG-level contribution require separate evidence and follow-up data. A mapping does not show that a learner, career or product has caused measurable progress.

Official UN goal icons identify the goals; their use does not imply UN endorsement. Photographs are representative, not evidence of FutureSkill outcomes.
