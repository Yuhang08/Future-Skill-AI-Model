About this prototype and how to read it

Search all 923 O*NET occupations. Career connections require genuine O*NET occupation task records. Broad title/SOC guesses are shown separately and never as verified links.

Occupation suggestions are separate from FutureSkill learning pathways. The learning product may support SDG 4.4 and 8.5; those are NOT automatically career goals.

Evidence availability varies by role. Employment outcomes, equitable access and broader SDG-level contribution require separate evidence and follow-up data. A mapping does not show that a learner, career or product has caused measurable progress.

Official UN goal icons identify the goals; their use does not imply UN endorsement. Photographs are representative, not evidence of FutureSkill outcomes.

Select a career for occupation-based SDG suggestions; any learning-pathway links appear in a separate section.
