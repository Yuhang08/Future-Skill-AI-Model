Answers, audio and history

Submitted answers are sent to the configured AI provider for feedback and held temporarily in server memory. The question-history database stores questions and career titles to reduce repeats, not answers or audio. Finished reports are saved in this browser.

Microphone use is optional. A recording is sent to the AI provider for transcription only after you choose to record and stop. You can edit the transcript before submitting.

Unfinished interviews cannot be resumed after leaving or reloading. Server-side answers are cleared on completion, on an expired-session request, or when the server restarts. Completed browser history remains separate.

The catalogue supplies occupation titles, not a sourced skills profile for every role. For those roles, questions are general practice based on the selected title; they do not assert verified occupational requirements.
