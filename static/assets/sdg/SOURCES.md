# SDG imagery sources

## Official goal icons

`sdg-1-icon.png` through `sdg-17-icon.png` are official United Nations goal
icons, downloaded from the UN Sustainable Development communications assets:

- https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/
- Source URL pattern:
  `https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/E-Inverted-Icons_WEB-NN.png`
- The existing Goal 8 asset uses the same source's `-1024x1024.png` variant.

The icons identify the goals; their use does not imply UN endorsement.
Keep the complete official artwork and do not recolor the source images.
